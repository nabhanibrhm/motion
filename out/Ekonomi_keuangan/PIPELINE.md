## What's in the news (end of October 2026)

- **Rupiah at a record low:** about Rp18.100 per dollar, with the weakest level ever at 18,183 on 28 October.
- **Stock market:** the IHSG (Indonesia's main stock index) crashed after MSCI warnings. On 16 October trading was halted at −10.7%, and the index fell below 5.000.
- **Online loans and paylater:** debt passed Rp156 triliun in February 2026.
- **Online gambling (judol):** Rp86,82 triliun changed hands in the first half of 2026, more than half of the deposits via QRIS.
- **Gold:** down from Rp3,17 juta per gram in January to about Rp2,54 juta.
- **Jobs and the middle class:** layoffs (PHK) are rising and the middle class is shrinking.
- **Wages:** the minimum wage for 2027 (UMP) is being decided in November.
- **Food prices:** the rice harvest from September to November is expected to be 12% lower than a year earlier.

- **#2 and #5** keep the "Bukan saran investasi" disclaimer (not investment advice).
- **#4** is strictly educational, with no site or game names, so YouTube doesn't restrict it.
