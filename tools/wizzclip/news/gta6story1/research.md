# GTA VI storyline: plot spine for "GTA 6 storyline explained" (3 parts)

Researched 26 Nov 2026, seven days after the 19 Nov 2026 release. Everything below is my own summary. Source codes are in brackets and listed in section 5.
**Two-source rule:** a beat counts as confirmed only when at least two independent outlets back it. GTABase [GB], Fextralife [FX] and gta.wiki [GW] are wikis. Gfinity [GF], Overdrive Brasil [OD], Gameblog [GBL], FandomWire [FW], Beebom [BB], Dexerto [DX], SVG [SVG], games.gg [GG] and Ayther [AY] are publications. Several GB pages from the same site count as one source.
**Not used:** pre-release leaks, rumours and fan theories (the undercover-cop theory, "they kill each other" and similar).
**Reliability caveat:** I could not reach IGN, GameSpot, Polygon, Eurogamer, PC Gamer, Game Rant or Kotaku. Kotaku returned 403, GTA Fandom returned 402, and searches surfaced no ending or plot guide from the others. The confirmations below therefore come from mid-tier outlets and wikis. Ending names and exact trigger thresholds differ between guides (see section 3), so in narration keep to the broad shape and avoid exact numbers.

## 2. The plot in chronological order

GTABase and Dexerto agree on the structure: a Prologue and five chapters [GB, DX, GF]. Chapter names are confirmed by [GB, DX]. Mission counts differ by source (91 to 118) because guides count optional side steps differently, so don't quote a number on screen.

### Prologue: "Prologue"
- Lucia, in prison, tells a counsellor how she got there. She left her baby **Tomás** at a café, robbed a loan shop, was caught after a chase, and lost the baby to child services. Jason is the father. They had broken up before her arrest. [SVG, OD, BB, GB char]

### Chapter 1: *Just Like Paradise* (Jason in the Keys)
- Jason runs jobs for **Brian Heder** with Cal and Donnie (Gone Fishing, Lab Fees, Clear Skies and others). [GF, BB]
- **Coming Home:** Jason picks Lucia up from Hamlet Correctional. A flashback shows their breakup. They celebrate at the Rusty Anchor and meet Kat's boyfriend **Mattia**. [GB, GF]
- **Donnie is killed** and the pair clean up after him (**Don Gone**). Brian retires. *SINGLE-SOURCE:* Gfinity says the Kingfishers set Donnie up. GB only shows the cleanup. [GF, BB, GB]
- Choice in **Hey Arnold!**: sparing or shooting Arnold affects Jason's criminal profile, and sparing him pays off later. [OD, GB endings]
- **Tourist Trap** (the inciting incident): Mattia offers an "easy" job, picking up a bag from a locker at a visitor centre. It is a trap. The pair are arrested, then corrupt **Captain Méndez** arrives for the bag and his men kill the honest officers. Jason is shot. Lucia escapes with him by boat to Cal, who takes him to Vice City Hospital. [GB, GF]
- **Home Sweet Home / Discharge Time:** masked men attack, Lucia's home is burned, and the pair flee the hospital. [GF, BB]

### Chapter 2: *Paradise Lost* (on the run in Port Gellhorn, Kelly County)
- **What's in the Bag?** They hide in a Port Gellhorn motel. On TV, Méndez publicly blames them for the attack. The bag holds a small statue and a **hard drive** they can't read yet. [GB, GF]
- Lucia nurses Jason and gets painkillers through local contacts (Play Nurse, Got Pills?), and they work with the mechanic **Moses**. [GF, BB]
- **Afternoon Delight:** they rob a strip-club owner (Vicki's boss). [GF, BB]
- **Checking Out:** the masked men find the motel, and the pair flee to Vice City. [GF, BB]

### Chapter 3: *Tits Ass Drug Money* (building a life in Vice City)
- **Welcome to Vice City:** they stay at an apartment of **Boobie Ike** and work for Boobie, Dre'Quan, Tyrone and others. Lucia reconnects with her mother **Mia** (Mami Knows Best). [GF, BB, GB char]
- **First Impressions / Credit Check:** they meet **Raul Bautista**, who recruits them for his crew. [BB, GF]
- **The Cartel Heist:** Raul's crew (with Nicole Park, Karim, Andrea and Felipe) robs **Sinfrontera National Bank**, which launders money for the **La Polilla Negra** cartel. It ends clean or in an escape firefight, depending on play. [GB, GF, BB]

### Chapter 4: *Vice City, Baby* (the big score, then the fall)
- **Lawyer Up:** **Valentina** sets them up at the Y Hotel and brings in the lawyer **Abe Gonzalez**. [GF, BB]
- Side arcs: Lucia goes undercover with Kordell Grant's crew at the gym (Making Gainz), and Jason guards telenovela star Andrés de León (and rescues Rafa in Child's Play). [GF, BB]
- **VCIA Takedown:** an airport heist ("VCIA" is the airport; GB doesn't spell it out). The job goes wrong: police swarm, Lucia fights Kordell on the plane, and Lucia and Raul land the plane in the bay. Raul, Valentina and Abe then try to sell the **hard drive**. Gfinity adds that the crew double-crosses its partners and that Karim and Kordell's crew die. *Those deaths are SINGLE-SOURCE.* [GB, GF, BB]
- **Sunset** (the midgame turn): Valentina says she is negotiating with buyers **Camila Fuentes** and **Giorgio Gallo**. The pair find **Raul** on his boat, beaten nearly to death. He says the cops did it and **Valentina gave him up**. Gfinity and Overdrive say Méndez tortured him and Jason and Lucia end his suffering. GB stops before confirming his death. A police and NOOSE assault follows, and they escape by sea. [GB, GF, OD]
- **Starting From Zero:** they get the drive back from Andrea, collect the statue, and are brought to **Giorgio Gallo's** penthouse by Mattia. Giorgio is the bag's real owner. [GB, GF]

### Chapter 5: *Top of the World, Ma* (working for the man they robbed)
- Giorgio explains that he used the drive to **blackmail Senator Camila Fuentes** to protect his cocaine smuggling, and he puts the pair to work. [GF, OD] *SINGLE-SOURCE:* Overdrive says he promises them a presidential pardon.
- **Bird in the Hand / Operation Savage Glory:** they raid a Coast Guard base to recover cargo and sink a rival cocaine shipment. Giorgio's specialists **Vinny** and **Liz** die. [GB, GF, OD]
- **Honest Abe / Unfinished Business:** Abe arranges a legal deal for a fee paid through BuckMe. This is the point of no return. [GB, BB]
- **Protective Custody:** Giorgio **betrays** them, and they are arrested separately. Prosecutor **Danica Howard** tells Lucia they will never be free without the drive. Lucia refuses to give up Jason, escapes her transport, and fights through the town of Hamlet to save Jason from corrupt cops at the precinct. They corner **Méndez** and must choose: **Shoot** him, or wait for him to offer a **Take Deal**. If the relationship is weak, they can break up at the start of this mission, which also changes the ending path. [GB, GF, OD, FX]
- **The End of the Road:** they assault Giorgio's tower by helicopter and on foot, recover the hard drive, and run for a plane south. Sheriff's units box them in **on a bridge in the Keys**, where the final choice comes. [GB, GF, OD, AY, FW]

## 3. The ending(s)

**What is confirmed (2+ sources):**
- There are **multiple endings**. GB and GBL count 8, FX lists 13 variants, and FW and OD say Rockstar described "a lot more than two". [GB, GBL, FX, FW, OD]
- Four things decide the ending: (1) story choices, above all **Shoot vs Take Deal with Méndez** in Protective Custody and the **bridge choice "Swim For It" vs "No Surrender"**; (2) the **relationship** level (top level "Hot Together"; a bad relationship allows a breakup); (3) each character's **criminal profile** (from Professional up to Psycho or Irredeemable); (4) money deposited into **BuckMe Family Savings**. [GB, FX, GG, OD, FW, GBL]
- After the credits you are sent back to just before the finale to try other endings. [GB, AY, FW] (FX says back to the start of Chapter 5 instead.)
- **No canonical ending.** No source names one, and OD says Rockstar hasn't named an official ending. **No source says which ending is most common.**

**Main endings (bridge path: Méndez shot, relationship intact):**

| Ending (name varies by guide) | Trigger | Outcome | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No Surrender** ("Bonnie & Clyde") | Choose No Surrender at the roadblock. Profile doesn't matter. | Jason drives at the barricade and **both die** together. Tomás is raised by Lucia's family (mother Mia). | GB, FX, OD, FW, GG |
| **Failed Escape** | Swim For It with a violent or psycho profile (Jason still armed) | Deputies **shoot both** before they reach the water. | GB, FX, FW, GG |
| **Jason Takes the Fall** | Swim For It, Hot Together, clean profile, savings not maxed | They jump holding hands, then **Jason lets go** so Lucia escapes with the drive. She takes the prosecutor's deal and raises Tomás. **Jason goes to prison**, and she later visits him with their son. | GB, FX, OD, FW, GG (GBL differs: says Jason later dies in prison) |
| **Two Years Later** (best) | Same as above plus **maximum Family Savings** (GB and FX: $2,000,000) and both Professional | Lucia uses the money (through Abe) to get Jason out. **The family reunites, free.** | GB, FX, GBL (FW describes a "fresh start" variant with the same requirements) |

**Other branches** (end at Giorgio's tower and never reach the bridge; described by GB, FX, GBL; FW and OD don't cover them):
- **Méndez's Deal:** take the deal while still a couple, and Méndez double-crosses them. The final shot of two skulls in a swamp implies both are dead. [GB, FX, GBL]
- **Defend Jason / The Sacrifice** (bad relationship): Jason is shot surrendering. If Lucia defends him, both die. If she stays down, she lives, is freed, and drifts back into crime. [GB, FX, GBL]
- **Jason escapes with Rocco** (broken up, deal taken, Professional profiles, savings threshold): Lucia surrenders and raises Tomás. Jason vanishes with the police dog Rocco and later leaves her a bag of money, watching from afar. [GB, FX, GBL]

**Script advice:** pitch the ending as "one bridge, one choice: Swim For It or No Surrender", tease the best ending, and avoid exact dollar thresholds, which differ across guides.

## 4. Suggested 3-part split

At about 110–130 words per part, 5–7 beats each.

### Part 1: "The easy score" (setup). Cliffhanger: the bag holds a hard drive.
1. Leonida, Florida-style. Lucia is in prison, and the Prologue twist is that she and Jason have a baby, **Tomás**, now taken into care. *(Prologue)*
2. Jason, ex-Army, does smuggling jobs for Brian in the Keys. *(Gone Fishing, Lab Fees)*
3. He picks Lucia up from prison, and the old flame restarts. *(Coming Home)*
4. Mattia offers an "easy" job: grab a bag from a locker. *(Tourist Trap)*
5. Trap. Corrupt **Captain Méndez** wants the bag, his men open fire, Jason is shot, and Lucia drags him out by boat. *(Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home, Discharge Time)*
6. Hiding in a Port Gellhorn motel, they open the bag and find a **hard drive**, while Méndez names them on TV. *(What's in the Bag?)*
- Cliffhanger line idea: "What's on this hard drive, and who wants them dead for it?"

### Part 2: "Vice City" (midgame turn). Cliffhanger: Raul is betrayed and Giorgio appears.
1. The masked men find the motel, so they flee to Vice City. *(Checking Out, Welcome to Vice City)*
2. They meet veteran bank robber **Raul Bautista**, their new mentor. *(First Impressions, Credit Check)*
3. They rob the bank that launders cartel money. *(The Cartel Heist)*
4. They pull an airport heist that goes wrong, while Valentina and Abe try to sell the drive. *(VCIA Takedown)*
5. Valentina betrays Raul. They find him beaten nearly to death, and police storm the docks. *(Sunset)*
6. On the run with nothing, they are brought to the drive's real owner, **Giorgio Gallo**. *(Starting From Zero)*
- Cliffhanger line idea: "The man whose drive they stole now offers them a job."

### Part 3: "End of the Road" (climax and endings). End on the comment question.
1. Giorgio blackmails a senator with the drive, and they do his dirty work. *(Bird in the Hand, Operation Savage Glory)*
2. Abe's deal, then Giorgio betrays them and they are arrested separately. *(Unfinished Business, Protective Custody)*
3. The prosecutor offers Lucia freedom if she gives up the drive (and Jason). She refuses and breaks Jason out. *(Protective Custody)*
4. Méndez is cornered: shoot him, or take his deal? *(Protective Custody)*
5. They storm Giorgio's tower and take back the drive. *(The End of the Road)*
6. Trapped on a bridge: **Swim For It** or **No Surrender**. Both die, Jason goes to prison, or, with enough saved, the family reunites. *(The End of the Road)*
- Comment question idea: "Kamu pilih Swim For It atau No Surrender?"

**Footage tip:** the cutscenes you need are at the start and end of: Prologue, Coming Home, Tourist Trap, What's in the Bag?, Checking Out, First Impressions, The Cartel Heist, VCIA Takedown, Sunset, Starting From Zero, Protective Custody and The End of the Road.
