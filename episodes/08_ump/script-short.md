# Gaji Naik 8%, Kok Tetap Kurang? (Short)

Episode #8 from [PIPELINE.md](../../content_idea/PIPELINE.md) (KASUS #08), in the "Kasus" format. **Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920**, Bahasa Indonesia, about **215 spoken words**, **about 95 s**, over the quiet music bed. Follows directly from episode 01 (inflasi). **Non-political:** no party, minister or union is praised or blamed; the 8% raise is a labelled simulation inside the union-proposed range, not a forecast.

Cast: **Nisa** (`wec`, F2) leads and explains (about two thirds of the words), **Muklis** (`mog`, M1) adds facts and the expert quote, **Beni** (`bog`, M4) asks; speed 1.0, widened pitch range. Lines in *[brackets]* are on-screen notes. Every on-screen figure carries a "Sumber: …" line. **[U#]** = sources table. Facts checked on 1 Nov 2026.

**Publish window:** before or right after the UMP 2027 announcement (target: by the end of November 2026 [U1]). If the official figure is out before rendering, update `kabar` and the hook (see "Before rendering").

### 1 · Hook (`hook`, LAPORAN MASUK)
*[Beni waves a phone headline "UMP 2027 NAIK 8%?" with a question mark; a cartoon money bag inflates. Nisa holds up a hand.]*

**BENI:** Katanya UMP tahun depan naik delapan persen! Akhirnya aku kaya!

**NISA:** Tunggu dulu, Ben. Gaji naik delapan persen, belum tentu kamu delapan persen lebih kaya.

### 2 · The news (`kabar`, BUKTI 1 · KABAR UMP)
*[Calendar page "NOVEMBER 2026" with "UMP 2027: BELUM DIPUTUSKAN" and "TARGET: PALING LAMBAT AKHIR NOVEMBER" (Kemnaker) [U1]. A range bar "USULAN SERIKAT BURUH 7,5%–9,5%" [U2]. On "Rumusnya": a formula card "INFLASI + PERTUMBUHAN EKONOMI + INDEKS ALFA" [U1]; stamp "GUBERNUR MENETAPKAN" [U2].]*

**NISA:** Faktanya, UMP dua ribu dua puluh tujuh belum diputuskan. Kemnaker menargetkan paling lambat akhir November.

**MUKLIS:** Serikat buruh minta naik tujuh setengah sampai sembilan setengah persen.

**BENI:** Rumusnya gimana?

**NISA:** Menghitung inflasi, pertumbuhan ekonomi, dan satu indeks bernama alfa. Keputusan akhirnya di gubernur.

### 3 · The maths (`hitung`, BUKTI 2 · SIMULASI)
*[Payslip: "RATA-RATA UMP 2026 · Rp3.503.220" [U3]; "SIMULASI +8%" (label "SIMULASI, BUKAN KEPUTUSAN") adds "+Rp280.258" and counts up to "Rp3.783.478". Beni grins.]*

**NISA:** Kita simulasi. Rata-rata UMP tahun ini sekitar tiga setengah juta. Kalau naik delapan persen, tambahnya dua ratus delapan puluh ribu.

**BENI:** Lumayan banget!

### 4 · The suspect (`inflasi`, TERSANGKA · HARGA)
*[A shopping basket labelled "Rp3,50 JT" (SETAHUN LALU) swells to "Rp3,62 JT" (SEKARANG); tag "+Rp115 RB" on "seratus lima belas ribu"; HUD "INFLASI SEP 2026 · 3,28% YOY" (BPS) [U4]. The +Rp280 RB coin stack from scene 3 loses a slice.]*

**MUKLIS:** Tapi harga juga naik. Inflasi September tiga koma dua delapan persen.

**NISA:** Belanjaan yang setahun lalu tiga setengah juta, sekarang sekitar seratus lima belas ribu lebih mahal. Tambahanmu kepotong duluan.

### 5 · Real wage (`riil`, BUKTI 3 · GAJI RIIL)
*[Equation card: "GAJI RIIL ≈ KENAIKAN GAJI − INFLASI"; "8% − 3,28% ≈ 4,7%" (small print: "perkiraan sederhana"). Then a side bar: "HARGA MAKANAN, MINUMAN & TEMBAKAU +4,37% (SEP 2026, BPS)" [U5], taller than the 3,28% bar.]*

**NISA:** Itu namanya gaji riil: kenaikan gaji dikurangi kenaikan harga. Naik delapan persen, riilnya cuma sekitar empat koma tujuh persen.

**MUKLIS:** Padahal harga makanan naik lebih cepat, empat koma empat persen.

### 6 · Lifestyle creep (`gaya`, SKENARIO · GAYA HIDUP)
*[Two lines on a chart: "GAJI" rising, "PENGELUARAN" rising faster and crossing it. Quote card: "“Gaji naik, tapi pengeluaran naik lebih cepat.” — Rista Maharani, CFP, seminar literasi keuangan OJK" [U6]. Three icons pop on the spoken words: coffee cup, a new instalment, a subscription.]*

**BENI:** Tapi kenapa tetap kerasa kurang?

**NISA:** Karena begitu gaji naik, pengeluaran ikut naik. Ngopi tiap hari, cicilan baru, langganan baru.

**MUKLIS:** Kata perencana keuangan di seminar OJK: gaji naik, tapi pengeluaran naik lebih cepat.

### 7 · What to do and close (`pelajaran`, VONIS)
*[Checklist: "HITUNG KENAIKAN RIIL, BUKAN ANGKA DI SLIP", "HARI GAJIAN: SISIHKAN KENAIKAN KE TABUNGAN DULU [U7]", "SISANYA BARU GAYA HIDUP". Paper end card: "KASUS DITUTUP", "KASUS #08 · GAJI NAIK", comment question, "SUMBER LENGKAP DI DESKRIPSI".]*

**NISA:** Jadi: hitung kenaikan riil, bukan angka di slip gaji. Begitu gajian, sisihkan sebagian kenaikan buat tabungan duluan.

**MUKLIS:** Sisanya baru buat gaya hidup.

**BENI:** Oke, nanti separuh kenaikanku langsung masuk tabungan.

**NISA:** Kasus ditutup! Gajimu tahun ini naik berapa, dan riilnya berapa? Tulis di komentar!

## Left out on purpose

- **A forecast of the actual UMP 2027 figure:** not decided; 8% is only a simulation inside the union range [U2]. The union's own maths (0,9 × (3,20% + 5,43%) ≈ 7,8%) is not shown, because the article does not show how 7,5–9,5% was derived.
- **Legal basis (PP 49/2025 vs the new labour law):** sources disagree on which one applies to 2027 [U1, U2]; the script says only "inflasi, pertumbuhan ekonomi, indeks alfa".
- **"Most of a low wage goes on food" / food share of spending:** no current official figure checked; the script only contrasts food inflation with headline inflation.
- **UMP coverage** (who actually earns the UMP, informal workers): out of scope for 95 s and not freshly sourced.
- **A fixed savings split** (e.g. 50/25/25 of a raise): media tips only, not an official rule. Beni's "separuh" is his own choice in the story, not advice.
- **Political framing** (government, party or union praise/blame): not used.
