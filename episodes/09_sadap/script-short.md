# Eks Kapolri Ditahan: Diduga Sadap Presiden (Short)

**HotWizz #01**, the first episode of the breaking-news theme (`config/themes/hotwizz.json`). **Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920**, Bahasa Indonesia, about **271 spoken words**, **about 105–110 s**. One **female narrator** (`wec`, F2) for the whole script. There is no cast; scenes are illustrated news graphics. Lines in *[brackets]* are on-screen notes. **[S#]** refers to the sources table. Facts checked on **4 Nov 2026, morning WIB**.

**Neutrality (mandatory):** the Geng Solo / kubu Prabowo reading is reported as what people say, with both readings given equal time, equal on-screen size and the same "belum terbukti" stamp. The social-media chronology is labelled unconfirmed and names no one; in particular, Irjen Slamet Uliandi (a witness, not a suspect) must never appear.

**Legal framing (mandatory):** every allegation is "diduga" or "tersangka"; the presumption of innocence is said aloud at the end; no motive is stated or hinted at, because police have not disclosed one [S3]. People are shown with the licensed photos listed under "Photo credits" (user request, 4 Nov 2026). Each photo appears only beside the claim about that person, never captioned as guilty. Eko Hadi Santoso has no freely licensed photo, so he appears as a silhouette with a name card. In `kubu`, Jokowi and Prabowo are shown at identical size and treatment. Do not use any of the items under "Do not use".

**Visual identity (HotWizz):** a breaking-news look. Near-black/navy stage (#0d0f16, #151a26), hot red #ff2d3d, flame orange #ff7a1a, signal yellow #ffd23f, white type. Mono/Oswald type. A small "HOTWIZZ" flame bug and a "BREAKING" red chip; the progress strip reads "HOTWIZZ #01 · <label>". Keep y 1480–1650 (captions), the bottom ~15% and the right ~120 px clear of art.

## Do not use

- "First former Kapolri ever detained" is false: Rusdihardjo (Kapolri 2000–01) was jailed for corruption (JPNN, 17 Mar 2009).
- "Dicopot dari KSP" was false when #01 was made (morning of 4 Nov). **Update:** around midday on 4 Nov the Istana announced Listyo's dismissal as KSP (see `episodes/10_ksp/script-short.md`, S1). #01 is unaffected because it never said he was removed.
- "Each charge carries more than 10 years" is false: the maximums are 10, 6 and 8 years.
- From the social-media chronology, use only the unnamed core (device detected, devices moved) and label it unconfirmed. Do not use: Ahrie resisting, being handcuffed or confessing on LSP's orders; Slamet Uliandi's or any other house named; "belasan jenderal"; Mako Brimob; a device "worth trillions"; or the death-penalty or makar calls.
- Do not use the resurfaced Jokowi "data intelijen parpol" video. It tilts toward one camp.
- No motive or "intellectual actor" speculation (Adrianus Meliala and ELSAM are opinion).
