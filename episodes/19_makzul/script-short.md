| # | Claim | Text |

| L1 | Pasal 7A: diberhentikan oleh MPR atas usul DPR … "apabila terbukti tidak lagi memenuhi syarat sebagai Presiden dan/atau Wakil Presiden". Pasal 7B (1) DPR must first ask MK; (3) request needs ≥2/3 of members present in a paripurna attended by ≥2/3 of members; (4) MK decides "paling lama sembilan puluh hari"; (5) if proven, DPR paripurna forwards to MPR; (6) MPR sits "paling lambat tiga puluh hari"; (7) MPR paripurna attended by ≥3/4 of members, approved by ≥2/3 of those present, after the President/VP may explain.
