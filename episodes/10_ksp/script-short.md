# Listyo Dicopot dari KSP: Sadapannya ke Mana? (Short)

**HotWizz #02**, the follow-up to #01 (`episodes/09_sadap`), using the `hotwizz` theme. Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, Bahasa Indonesia. About **242 spoken words**, **122.8 s** of voice. One **female narrator** (`wec`, F2) throughout, with no cast. Lines in *[brackets]* are on-screen notes, and **[S#]** points to the sources table. Facts were checked on **4 Nov 2026, about 13:30 WIB** and rechecked at **about 15:00 WIB** (Reuters 14:11 WIB still reports no comment from Listyo and no known motive). Script approved by the user on 4 Nov 2026, with the Yusril line added after the 15:00 recheck.

**Audio:** no music bed in the render (`audio.bed: null`). At the user's request (4 Nov 2026), the background sound is added inside TikTok at upload.

**Pacing (user request):** keep the pace even throughout, and especially in the hook: no fast→slow or slow→fast. Supertonic rushes short isolated chunks (e.g. text split by `[pause]` markers) and paces unevenly across commas, so this script uses no `[pause]` markers, puts the hook in one chunk, and uses few commas. Result: chunk rates 5.67–6.53 syll/s (3.7% spread, vs 13.7% in #01); hook sentences within 0.7%. Supertonic noise is unseeded, so if the voice cache is lost, re-synthesise and re-measure.

**Neutrality (mandatory):** the political readings get equal time, equal on-screen size and the same "BELUM TERBUKTI" stamp. In `kemana`, Jokowi and Prabowo appear at identical size and treatment, each linked to a factual tie: Listyo was Jokowi's ex-aide, and Ahrie was Prabowo's aide. Neither tie is evidence of anything. No one who is not a suspect is named as involved.

**Legal framing (mandatory):**
- "tersangka" throughout.
- The presumption of innocence is said aloud.
- No motive is stated or hinted at, and there is no "who ordered it" or intellectual-actor speculation; "apa peran masing-masing" asks only about the three suspects.
- The Palace's "makar" reading is attributed to the Palace and set beside the actual charges.
- "DIBERHENTIKAN" goes on the name card, never across a face.

**Visual identity:** HotWizz, as in #01. Dark stage #0d0f16/#151a26, hot red #ff2d3d, flame orange #ff7a1a, signal yellow #ffd23f, white type, Oswald/JetBrains Mono/Montserrat. The progress strip reads "HOTWIZZ #02 · <label>". Keep y 1480–1650 (captions), the bottom ~15% and the right ~120 px clear.
