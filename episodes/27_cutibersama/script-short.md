## Belum pasti (ask before using)

- **24 Desember 2026** is a cuti bersama day (SKB 2026; bisnis.com, eraspace). The UU is in force by then, so reading 118(6) it should not cut leave if the firm gives the day. No official or outlet has said this: only usable as "menurut bunyi pasalnya…" or as an on-screen question.
