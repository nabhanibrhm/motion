# Diskon 11.11: Hemat atau Boros? (Short)

Idea #11 from [PIPELINE.md](../../content_idea/PIPELINE.md) (KASUS #09), in the "Kasus" format. **Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920**, Bahasa Indonesia, about **190 spoken words**, **about 90 s**, over the quiet music bed. **Shopping decision, not debt** (that was paylater, KASUS #04). **No bank, platform, shop or product brand is named or drawn on screen**: the voucher is a generic "PROMO BANK" modelled on real terms [D1]; Beni's cart, list and receipts are a labelled **simulasi**, without shipping or other fees. Never claim that promos are fake or that prices were raised; the law only *forbids* that [D3].

Cast: **Nisa** (`wec`, F2) explains and leads the maths, **Muklis** (`mog`, M1) brings the facts, **Beni** (`bog`, M4) is the shopper; speed 1.0, widened pitch range. "Populix" is said "popiuliks" (`say`). Lines in *[brackets]* are on-screen notes. Every on-screen figure carries a "Sumber: …" line. **[D#]** = sources table. Facts checked on 3 Nov 2026.

**Publish window:** before 11 Nov 2026 (the promo terms in [D1] run 25 Okt–12 Nov 2026).

**TikTok safety:** no sensitive words; the caption leads with the question, not a sales pitch. See memory note on censored words.

### 1 · Hook (`hook`, LAPORAN MASUK)
*[Beni holds a phone with a big "11.11" banner and a green bubble "HEMAT Rp100 RB!"; Nisa raises an eyebrow, a magnifier over a paper receipt.]*

**BENI:** Sebelas-sebelas! Aku hemat seratus ribu!

**NISA:** Hemat? Coba kita cek struknya dulu, Ben.

### 2 · The terms (`syarat`, BUKTI 1 · SYARAT PROMO)
*[A generic voucher card "PROMO BANK 11.11 · CONTOH": "DISKON 10%" → "MIN. BELANJA Rp1.000.000" → "POTONGAN MAKS. Rp120.000", small "Sumber: S&K promo bank 11.11, per 3 Nov 2026 · bukan endorsement" [D1]. On "tujuh": Beni's list "DAFTAR BELANJA Rp700.000" slides in, a red gap "KURANG Rp300.000".]*

**MUKLIS:** Contoh promo bank minggu ini: diskon sepuluh persen, minimal belanja satu juta, potongan maksimal seratus dua puluh ribu.

**BENI:** Padahal belanjaanku cuma tujuh ratus ribu.

### 3 · Padding the cart (`keranjang`, BUKTI 2 · KERANJANG)
*[A progress bar "MIN. BELANJA" filling from Rp700.000 to Rp1.000.000 as three generic items (mug, socks, cable) drop into a cart; on "pas" the bar turns green "SYARAT TERPENUHI". On "jebakannya" the three items get a red tag "DI LUAR DAFTAR". Label "SIMULASI".]*

**BENI:** Ya udah, aku tambah tiga ratus ribu biar pas satu juta.

**NISA:** Nah, di situ jebakannya. Barang tambahan itu tadinya nggak ada di daftarmu.

### 4 · Two receipts (`struk`, BUKTI 3 · DUA STRUK)
*[Two paper receipts side by side. Right, "DENGAN PROMO": SUBTOTAL Rp1.000.000, DISKON 10% −Rp100.000, BAYAR Rp900.000. Left, "RENCANA AWAL": Rp700.000. On "dua ratus ribu": a red bracket between them "+Rp200.000 DARI RENCANA". Label "SIMULASI · TANPA ONGKIR".]*

**NISA:** Kita hitung. Satu juta dipotong sepuluh persen, kamu bayar sembilan ratus ribu. Rencana awalmu tujuh ratus ribu.

**BENI:** Lho, berarti?

**NISA:** Kamu keluar dua ratus ribu lebih banyak.

### 5 · Why (`kenapa`, TERSANGKA · RASA HEMAT)
*[Beni confused. A big stat card "> 1/2 ORANG INDONESIA CENDERUNG BELANJA IMPULSIF", "Sumber: survei Populix, 2023 (>1.000 responden)" [D5]. On "potongannya": a scale (balance) where a giant "−Rp100 RB" sticker outweighs a small "TOTAL Rp900 RB" tag, then flips.]*

**BENI:** Kok aku nggak sadar, ya?

**MUKLIS:** Kamu nggak sendirian. Survei Populix tahun dua ribu dua puluh tiga: lebih dari separuh orang Indonesia cenderung belanja impulsif.

**NISA:** Promo bikin kita fokus ke potongannya, bukan ke total bayarnya.

### 6 · Check first (`cek`, BUKTI 4 · CEK DULU)
*[A law card "UU 8/1999 PERLINDUNGAN KONSUMEN · PASAL 11: DILARANG MENAIKKAN HARGA SEBELUM OBRAL" [D3]; a small price-history line chart labelled "CEK RIWAYAT HARGA". On "syaratnya": a checklist ticking one per word: MINIMAL BELANJA · BATAS POTONGAN · KUOTA · METODE BAYAR · ONGKIR.]*

**MUKLIS:** Undang-undang perlindungan konsumen melarang penjual menaikkan harga sebelum obral. Jadi cek riwayat harganya.

**NISA:** Baca juga syaratnya: minimal belanja, batas potongan, kuota, metode bayar, dan ongkir.

### 7 · Verdict (`pelajaran`, VONIS)
*[Card "BANDINGKAN: TOTAL BAYAR vs KEBUTUHAN AWAL", crossed-out "HARGA CORET". Beni drags the three extra items out; cart back to Rp700.000. Paper end card: "KASUS DITUTUP", "KASUS #09 · DISKON 11.11", "PERNAH NAMBAH BARANG BIAR VOUCHER KEPAKAI?", "SUMBER LENGKAP DI DESKRIPSI".]*

**NISA:** Jadi, bandingkan total bayar dengan kebutuhan awalmu, bukan dengan harga coret. Kalau barangnya memang butuh, baru beda cerita.

**BENI:** Oke, keranjangku balik ke tujuh ratus ribu.

**NISA:** Kasus ditutup! Kamu pernah nambah barang cuma biar voucher kepakai? Tulis di komentar!
